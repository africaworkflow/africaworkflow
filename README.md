## 🚀 Featured Project

### Skanema — Restaurant Ordering & Management Platform

A SaaS platform designed to help restaurants manage orders and communicate with customers through WhatsApp.

In many African markets, customers are already familiar with using WhatsApp to communicate with businesses and place orders. Rather than asking them to download another application, Skanema builds on this existing behavior to make online ordering more accessible and convenient.

The platform enables customers to discover menus, place orders, share delivery locations, and complete payments through a WhatsApp-centered experience, while giving businesses tools to manage orders and streamline their operations.
